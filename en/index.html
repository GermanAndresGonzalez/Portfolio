About Me

Background & Profile

I am an English–Spanish professional translator with more than 20 years of experience and a software developer in training. Throughout my career, I have collaborated with clients across diverse countries and cultures, translating technical manuals, safety protocols, and industrial documentation for agricultural machinery and automotive enterprises. This journey has honed my eye for detail, clear communication, and adaptability to multidisciplinary teams.

Currently, I am studying at UTN while building projects in Python, C#, C++, and databases, alongside microcontroller programming. While my developer journey is just getting started, my translation background is a strong asset: I am accustomed to demanding deadlines, adept at navigating bilingual technical documentation, and skilled at authoring clear documentation that ensures code is maintainable.

I am driven by continuous learning and eager to contribute to teams where I can bring a meticulous, thoughtful, and communication-driven perspective.
